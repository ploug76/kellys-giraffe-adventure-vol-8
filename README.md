# Kelly's Giraffe Adventure Vol. 8: Ocean Giraffe: Pip's Deep Dive

Kelly's giraffe hits the beach! Team up with Pip the sea turtle to wade through tide pools, swim through coral caves and bonk 4 silly sea bosses across 8 painted levels to win back Peter's birthday present from Captain Crab.

Play: https://ploug76.github.io/kellys-giraffe-adventure-vol-8/

A five8nine game. Sequel to Kelly's Giraffe Adventure Vol. 2: The Rhino Rumble.

Controls: swipe and hold anywhere to move, swipe up or JUMP to jump, ACTION for head bonk, neck ram (double-tap), tongue slurp, ring swing, neck grab (hold UP at a ledge) and neck pound (DOWN+ACTION in the air). In deep water you ride Pip the sea turtle: swipe to swim, JUMP to paddle up or leap out, DIVE to swoop down. Keyboard: arrows/WASD, Z/Space jump, X action, V dive, C ram, P pause, M sound. Controllers: stick/D-pad move, A jump, X action, B dive, Y ram, Start pause.

All art is drawn; music and sound are synthesized in the browser. Happy Birthday, Kelly!
